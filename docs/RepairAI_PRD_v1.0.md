# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# AI-Powered Electronics Service & Repair Platform (RepairAI)

**Version:** 1.0 (MVP Foundation)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Executive Summary

RepairAI is an AI-powered marketplace and field service management
platform for electronics and appliance repair. The platform connects
customers, certified technicians, vendors, warehouses, and
administrators through a unified ecosystem.

Unlike traditional repair marketplaces, RepairAI combines AI diagnosis,
technician management, spare parts management, warranty, AMC, inventory,
live tracking, and analytics into one platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Vision

Build the most trusted AI-powered electronics service platform that
delivers fast, transparent, and reliable repairs.

# 3. Mission

Digitize electronics servicing by combining AI, automation, and
certified technicians into one scalable ecosystem.

# 20. Conclusion

RepairAI is envisioned as an AI-first electronics service ecosystem
rather than a simple booking platform. The MVP focuses on high-demand
appliance categories while providing a scalable architecture for AI
diagnostics, technician operations, inventory management, and long-term
enterprise expansion.
