# 🏠 Home Repair & Maintenance Platform (Phase 1)

> **Tagline:** *Book Trusted Home Repair Services Anytime, Anywhere.*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 📖 Introduction

The Home Repair & Maintenance Platform is a digital marketplace built to
simplify everyday household repair and maintenance services.

Today, if a switchboard stops working, a fan stops rotating, an AC stops
cooling, a tap starts leaking, or a geyser stops working, people usually
search for local technicians through friends, shops, or online listings.
This process is time-consuming, lacks transparency, and provides no
guarantee about technician quality or pricing.

This platform solves that problem by connecting customers with verified
technicians through a single mobile application.

Customers can book services in minutes, track technician status,
purchase affordable service plans, receive digital invoices, and
maintain a complete repair history. Vendors receive verified jobs, while
the admin manages the complete ecosystem.

**Phase 1 focuses only on creating a robust service booking platform. AI
features will be added in a future phase.**

# 💳 Subscription Model

## 🥉 Basic Plan (3 Months)

-   3 Home Visits
-   Labour Charges Included
-   Spare Parts Charged Separately
-   1 Free AC Cleaning

## 🥈 Standard Plan (6 Months)

-   6 Home Visits
-   Labour Charges Included
-   Spare Parts Charged Separately
-   2 Free AC Cleanings
-   Priority Booking

## 🥇 Premium Plan (12 Months)

-   12 Home Visits
-   Labour Charges Included
-   Spare Parts Charged Separately
-   4 Free AC Cleanings
-   Priority Support
-   Faster Technician Assignment

### Business Rule

The subscription only covers **technician labour charges** within the
plan limits.

If any spare parts, accessories, or external materials are required
during repair, those charges are always paid separately by the customer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 💰 Pay Per Visit

Customers who do not purchase a subscription can still use the platform.

-   ₹199 Home Visit Charge
-   Includes technician visit and inspection
-   Labour charges and spare parts are quoted after inspection
-   Customer approves estimate before work begins

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🛠️ Vendor Registration & Verification

1.  Vendor registers using OTP.
2.  Fills personal and professional details.
3.  Uploads Aadhaar, selfie and required documents.
4.  Selects services and working area.
5.  Admin reviews the application.

Status: - Pending - Approved - Rejected (with reason)

Only approved vendors can receive bookings.

# 📋 Complete Booking Flow

Customer selects service

↓

Chooses address

↓

Selects preferred date & time

↓

Booking request created

↓

Nearby vendors receive request

↓

Vendor accepts booking

↓

Customer receives technician details

↓

Vendor clicks **Start Journey**

↓

Customer receives **Technician On The Way**

↓

Vendor arrives

↓

Inspection completed

↓

Estimate shared

↓

Customer approves estimate

↓

Repair starts

↓

Vendor uploads before & after photos

↓

Job marked: - Completed - Pending - Cancelled (reason required)

↓

Invoice generated

↓

Customer provides rating and review
