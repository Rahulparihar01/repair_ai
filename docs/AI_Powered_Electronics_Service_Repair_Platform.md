# ⚡ AI-Powered Electronics Service & Repair Platform (RepairAI)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🧠 1. Introduction

A comprehensive **AI-powered Electronics Service & Repair Platform**
designed to modernize and digitize the electronics repair industry by
connecting customers, certified technicians, service centers, vendors,
and manufacturers on a single intelligent platform.

The platform enables users to **book repair services, installations,
maintenance, and inspections** for a wide range of electronic devices
while leveraging AI for fault diagnosis, cost estimation, technician
assistance, predictive maintenance, and intelligent service
recommendations.

Unlike traditional service marketplaces, the platform combines
**AI-driven diagnostics, technician management, inventory management,
spare parts marketplace, warranty tracking, Annual Maintenance Contracts
(AMC), real-time tracking, and enterprise service management** into one
unified ecosystem.

The goal is to become the **complete digital infrastructure for
electronics servicing**, providing faster repairs, transparent pricing,
genuine spare parts, and an exceptional customer experience.
